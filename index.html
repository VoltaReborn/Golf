Rules

Goal

Finish 9 or 18 holes with the lowest total score.

Setup

Each player gets either 4 cards in a 2x2 grid or 6 cards in a 3x2 grid. All cards start face down. You may look at the bottom two cards based on the selected look rule.

Turn

  • Draw from the deck, then either discard it and flip one face-down card, or replace one face-down card with it.
  • Or take the top discard card and replace one face-down card with it.
  • Face-up cards are locked. They cannot be replaced.

Scoring

Aces are 1, number cards are face value, Jacks and Queens are 10, Kings are 0. Exact pairs cancel. Jokers are wild during scoring and are assigned automatically to minimize points.
